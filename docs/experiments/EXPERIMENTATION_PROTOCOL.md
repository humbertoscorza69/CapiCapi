# EXPERIMENTATION PROTOCOL v1.0

Status: DRAFT
Date: 2026-09-04
Truth level: PROVISIONAL

## Purpose

CAPICAPI treats creative, product, and manufacturing changes as experiments. Experiments must identify the hypothesis, variables, result metrics, and decision impact.

## Experiment Types

- print experiment
- visual language experiment
- product/rarity experiment
- content experiment
- sales page experiment
- packaging experiment
- operational workflow experiment

## Required Fields

Each experiment requires:

- experiment ID
- owner
- hypothesis
- start date
- end date
- controlled variables
- changed variables
- success metrics
- data source
- status
- result
- decision impact

## Truth Labels

Use these labels consistently:

- KNOWN
- MEASURED
- ESTIMATED
- ASSUMED
- PROVISIONAL
- TBD — REQUIRES PHYSICAL TEST
- TBD — REQUIRES SLICER DATA
- TBD — REQUIRES MARKET DATA

## Viral Score And Commercial Score

Separate attention from business value.

Viral score may consider:

- 1-second hold
- 3-second hold
- average watch time
- completion rate
- rewatch
- likes
- comments
- shares
- saves

Commercial score may consider:

- profile visits
- follows
- store clicks
- add-to-cart
- purchase
- revenue
- contribution margin

A high-view creative is not automatically the best commercial creative.

## Decision Rules

An experiment can lead to:

- adopt
- reject
- revise and retest
- collect more data
- escalate for decision

Do not upgrade assumptions to rules without evidence.

## Open Risks

- Early datasets may be too small for confident conclusions.
- Platform metrics may change over time.
- Commercial tracking may lag viral performance data.
