## Scope And Limits

Twenty separate built-in image_gen.imagegen calls generated M01-A / M01-B through M10-A / M10-B. Each used the exact approved shared-plus-family prompt, without image references, editing, or replacement. The originals are 1254 x 1254 PNGs. All twenty Drive copies were checksum-verified against their local originals.

Procedural independence did not guarantee the requested independent interpretations: most pairs differ modestly, and recurring generic face/body language narrows the exploration. This is a limited-diversity result, not a claim that twenty materially different character constructions were achieved. No result is selected or approved.

All samples have visible prompt deviations, particularly segmented digits and fine surface grain; M01, M03, and M09 also miss the upright body brief (six images). M01-B faces the opposite direction from most samples. Framing is generally tighter than requested, and camera/expression vary. Originals were not normalized by editing; preserve these as comparison limitations.

Several images read as hippo-like or generic animal mascots rather than unmistakably capybara-derived. M09-A/B show a conceptual accessory-platform dead-end concern: a shoulder dome encloses the crown/neck instead of preserving open transitions. No family is automatically rejected and no next-generation work is authorized.

Naked identity is judged from the existing hero. Costumed identity survival, expression range, family scalability, accessories, and FDM viability are conceptual estimates only. No costume, alternate silhouette, turnaround, expression sheet, palette, Series 01, or 3D geometry was generated. Rear views, actual fit, support-free behavior, strength, Color-Z/purge, and dimensions remain untested.

## Reading The Criteria

Scores remain separate: 1 = weak/contradicted, 3 = mixed, 5 = strong apparent evidence; higher is favorable. There is no total, weighting, or automatic ranking. Scores of 4+ flag a relative strength in that criterion, 1-2 flag a concern, and 3 is mixed. A high conceptual dressing score is not proof of printable fit or future identity. Raw observations and uncertainties take priority.
