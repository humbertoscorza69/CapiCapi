# CONTENT BIBLE v1.0

Status: DRAFT
Date: 2026-09-04
Truth level: PROVISIONAL

## Purpose

The Content Bible defines CAPICAPI's repeatable content language before automation. It supports human-approved creative experiments and later an AI-assisted growth engine.

## Content Principles

- Product truth comes first.
- Physical process is part of the story.
- Show actual characters, prints, tests, failures, and improvements.
- Do not imply scarcity, quality, or production capacity that is not true.
- Every creative is an experiment.
- Human approval is mandatory before publishing.

## Content Pillars

Initial pillars:

- character reveal
- print process
- engineering test
- rarity/mystery
- behind-the-scenes manufacturing
- design iteration
- packaging/fulfillment later
- collection/completion later

## Tone

CAPICAPI should sound:

- precise
- warm
- playful in character moments
- honest about testing
- curious about process

Avoid:

- fake urgency
- fake scarcity
- unverified quality claims
- pretending prototypes are production units
- generic AI-generated captions

## Mandatory Approval Data

Before a creative can be approved, the owner should see:

- creative preview
- hook
- caption
- hashtags
- audio strategy
- platform
- scheduled time
- experiment hypothesis
- character
- product claim review

## Future Telegram Approval

Future owner actions:

- APPROVE
- EDIT
- REJECT

Nothing auto-publishes initially without approval.

## Product Claim Rules

Allowed only if true:

- "prototype"
- "test print"
- "first palette test"
- "limited edition"
- "sold out"
- "one printer"
- "support-free"

If a claim depends on physical results, it is blocked until measured.

## Open Risks

- Content automation may drift away from product truth if not constrained by schemas.
- Trend-chasing can dilute visual IP.
- Strong viral hooks may create unsupported product claims.
