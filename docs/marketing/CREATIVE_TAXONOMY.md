# CREATIVE TAXONOMY v1.0

Status: DRAFT
Date: 2026-09-04

## Purpose

This taxonomy defines structured creative variables for content experiments. It allows CAPICAPI to learn what drives attention and commerce separately.

- rarity_claims

## Outcome Metrics

Attention:

- 1-second hold
- 3-second hold
- average watch time
- completion rate
- rewatch
- likes
- comments
- shares

Commercial:

- profile visits
- follows
- store clicks
- add-to-cart
- purchase
- revenue

## QA Fields

Every creative should include:

- factual claim status
- brand consistency status
- product visibility status
- approval status
- experiment ID
- notes

## Open Risks

- Platforms expose metrics differently.
- Attribution from social creative to purchase may be incomplete.
- Early testing may favor novelty over durable brand value.
